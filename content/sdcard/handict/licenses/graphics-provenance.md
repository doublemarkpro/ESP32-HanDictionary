# Graphics pack provenance

Pack: cream-pastel-v1, created for ESP32-HanDictionary, 2026-09-11.

The six home illustrations and three study/foliage decorations are AI-generated
using the built-in OpenAI image generation tool, with the project's approved UI
concept images as style references. Original images and exact prompts are in
`assets/graphics/source/raster` and `assets/graphics/prompts.json` in the repository.
No dictionary pages, licensed dictionary text, instructional audio or trademarks
are included in this graphics pack. Illustration clock hands are decorative.

Weather, control, background and panel vectors are original project artwork.
The project provides the original vectors and generator under its MIT license;
retain the repository LICENSE when redistributing those sources. AI images are
provided as generated, without a claim of exclusivity or a third-party-rights warranty.

All device PNGs are text-free. Chinese lettering in repository-only overview sheets
uses Source Han Sans SC; its SIL OFL license is also included in this licenses folder.
